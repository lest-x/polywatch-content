> **TL;DR**
> - Apple smart glasses now expected to debut at WWDC 2027 and ship later that year
> - Delay linked to privacy and technical risks
> - Apple is pushing Samsung and LG for steep OLED price cuts on iPhone 18

## 📈 Smart glasses timeline update

| Previous expectation | New report |
|---|---|
| Possible 2026 or early 2027 | Late 2027 (WWDC reveal + later availability) |
| Reason | Privacy risks and readiness concerns |

According to supply-chain reports dated July 27, Apple has delayed its first consumer smart glasses. The company now plans to present the product at WWDC in June 2027 before opening sales later in the year. Privacy-related challenges are cited as a key factor in the revised schedule.

## 📱 iPhone 18 OLED cost pressure

Apple is simultaneously pressing its display suppliers for significant price reductions:

- Target: ~20% lower cost on OLED panels for the iPhone 18 series
- Challenge: Newer M16-related panel technology is more expensive
- Suppliers: Samsung Display and LG Display

The request comes even as component costs in other areas (especially memory) have driven recent price increases across Apple’s hardware lineup.

## ✅ What it means for buyers

- No smart glasses in 2026 or early 2027
- iPhone 18 pricing will depend on how successful Apple is at extracting display cost cuts
- Foldable iPhone development continues on a separate track

## 🗓️ Key dates to watch

- WWDC 2027: Expected smart glasses announcement window
- September 2026: Likely iPhone 18 family launch
- Ongoing: Supply-chain updates on foldable iPhone and further AI hardware
