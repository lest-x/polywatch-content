> **TL;DR**

> - Siri AI will be a core feature
> - Designed for tabletop and possibly wall-mounted placement
> - Aimed at kitchens, living rooms, and bedrooms

## 📈 The report

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports that Apple is working on an all-new smart home hub. The device is targeted for release sometime between October 2026 and early 2027.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Expected window | October 2026 – early 2027 |
| Core feature | Siri AI |
| Form factors | Tabletop (confirmed concept) + possible wall versions |
| Placement | Kitchen, living room, bedroom |

## 🏠 What we know so far

The hub is designed to sit in multiple rooms of the home and act as a central control point powered by Apple’s latest Siri AI capabilities. Gurman’s report describes both tabletop and additional form-factor options under consideration.

This would mark Apple’s most significant dedicated smart-home hardware push since the original HomePod.

## ✅ Why it matters

- Strengthens Apple’s position in the smart-home category
- Gives Siri AI a prominent always-available hardware presence
- Could compete more directly with Amazon Echo and Google Nest hubs

## 🗓️ Timeline to watch

- Further supply-chain or Gurman updates in the coming months
- Possible mention at a fall Apple event
- Official announcement window still open
