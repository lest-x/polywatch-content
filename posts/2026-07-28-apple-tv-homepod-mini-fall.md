## 📈 The latest from Gurman

According to Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman, refreshed versions of the Apple TV and HomePod mini are nearly ready. Apple is planning to release them in the fall of 2026.

| Device | Status | Key upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Apple TV | Nearly ready | Faster chip + Siri AI |
| HomePod mini | Nearly ready | Faster chip + Siri AI |

## 📺 What to expect

Both products are expected to receive meaningful processor upgrades that enable deeper Siri AI features. The timing aligns with Apple’s typical fall hardware cadence, potentially alongside or shortly after the iPhone 18 series.

Earlier rumors had already pointed to a new Apple TV with improved performance; today’s report adds confirmation that the HomePod mini is also close.

## ✅ Why it matters

- Brings Siri AI to two of Apple’s most common living-room and whole-home devices
- Gives existing HomePod mini owners a clear upgrade path
- Strengthens Apple’s entertainment and smart-home ecosystem ahead of the holiday season
