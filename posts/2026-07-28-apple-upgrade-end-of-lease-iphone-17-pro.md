> **TL;DR**
> - After 24 months you do **not** automatically own the iPhone 17 Pro
> - Three choices: Upgrade + return, Return and leave, or Buy it out
> - Buyout cost is roughly $330–$350 after paying ~$768 in monthly payments
> - You get a 6-month decision window before Klarna forces the buyout

## 📈 The situation

You leased an **iPhone 17 Pro 256GB** (list price $1,099) through Apple Upgrade for 24 months at the typical rate of **$31.99 per month**.

After making all 24 payments you have paid approximately **$767.76**.  
At this point the lease term ends and you must choose what to do next.

## ✅ Your three official options

| Option | What you do | What you pay extra | Result |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|--------|
| **1. Upgrade** | Return the 17 Pro and start a new lease on a newer iPhone | New monthly payments on the next device | Keep getting the latest phone |
| **2. Return & Leave** | Send the phone back in good condition | Nothing more | Exit the program completely |
| **3. Buy Out** | Pay the Purchase Option Fee via Klarna | ≈ $330–$350 (+ tax) | You own the iPhone 17 Pro forever |

### How the buyout is calculated
Purchase Option Fee = Original list price − total monthly payments already made − any remaining trade-in credit

For the iPhone 17 Pro 256GB this usually lands in the **$330–$350** range (before tax).

## ⏳ The 6-month decision window

Apple and Klarna give you **six extra months** after the official end date to decide.

- You continue paying the monthly amount during this period.
- If you received a trade-in credit at the start, your payments may increase once the original term ends (the credit only applied to the first 24 months).
- If you still take no action after the six months, Klarna will automatically charge the full Purchase Option Fee and you become the owner.

## ⚠️ Important rules

- The phone must be returned in good working condition (normal wear is fine; heavy damage can trigger extra fees).
- You never own the device unless you pay the buyout fee.
- Early termination (before 24 months) is possible but requires paying the remaining months + returning the device, or buying it out early.

## 🗓️ Bottom line

Leasing the iPhone 17 Pro 256GB keeps your monthly cost low ($31.99), but ownership is optional.  
After two years you either hand the phone back, upgrade to the next model, or pay roughly another $340 to keep it.

Choose the path that matches how long you actually want to keep the device.
