> **TL;DR**
> - 🍎 Apple Upgrade launches July 28 with Klarna — true device leasing (not installment financing)
> - Lower monthly payments but no AppleCare+ included; residual/buyout fees possible
> - You must fully wipe all personal data and files before returning or upgrading any device

## 📈 What’s happening

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| [Apple Upgrade Program](https://www.apple.com) | New Klarna-backed leasing for most iPhone, Mac, iPad & Apple Watch |
| Launch date | July 28, 2026 (U.S. only at start) |
| Terms | 24 months (iPhone/Watch) · 36 months (Mac/iPad) |
| Key change | Replaces new sign-ups for iPhone Upgrade Program |

Apple is launching **Apple Upgrade**, a major shift from traditional financing to true leasing in partnership with Klarna. The goal is lower monthly payments to offset recent hardware price hikes driven by memory shortages. Eligible devices exclude entry-level models such as Apple Watch SE, base iPad, iPhone 16, and MacBook Neo.

## 💰 Cost & structure

Exact monthly prices and APRs are not yet public (they go live with the program on July 28). Based on Klarna’s existing Upgrade Financing and the Bloomberg report:

- Monthly payments are designed to be lower than current Apple financing or the old iPhone Upgrade Program.
- Structure is lease-like: monthly payments + possible residual/balloon payment if you keep the device.
- Soft credit check required (does not hurt your score).
- Early payoff, mid-term upgrade, or return may trigger additional fees.
- **No AppleCare+ is included** — you must buy protection separately.

For context, Klarna’s current Apple storefront shows example rates such as ~$17.99/mo for certain iPad models (0–29.99% APR depending on credit). Expect similar “low monthly + residual” math.

## ✅ Pros

- Noticeably lower monthly payments than buying outright or using standard 0% financing.
- Flexible end-of-term options: upgrade, return, or buy out.
- Covers phones, tablets, computers, and watches under one program.
- Soft credit pull only.
- Helps Apple keep customers in the upgrade cycle without carrying the full loan risk.

## ❌ Cons

- You do not automatically own the device.
- Residual/buyout cost if you want to keep it.
- Possible fees for early actions.
- No bundled AppleCare+.
- Entry-level devices excluded.
- Interest may apply depending on your credit.
- Total cost of ownership can be higher if you keep the device long-term.

## 🔒 Personal data and files — critical steps

**You are fully responsible for your data.** Apple and Klarna will not back up, transfer, or recover anything.

Before returning or upgrading a device you must:

1. Back up everything (iCloud + computer or external drive).
2. Transfer data to the new device (Quick Start is ideal).
3. Sign out of iCloud / Apple Account.
4. Turn off Find My / Activation Lock.
5. Erase all content and settings (factory reset).
6. Remove any physical SIM and unpair Apple Watch.

Apple’s official trade-in and iPhone Upgrade Program terms state that data recovery is not provided and neither Apple nor the trade-in partner accepts liability for lost files. Treat Apple Upgrade the same way. Once the device is wiped and returned, your data is gone from that hardware forever (iCloud content remains in your account).

## 🗓️ Who this is good for

- People who upgrade every 1–2 years and prefer lower monthly cash flow.
- Users comfortable treating devices like a subscription/lease.
- Anyone who already buys AppleCare+ separately.

Skip it if you prefer to own the device outright, want AppleCare bundled, or mainly buy entry-level models.
