> **TL;DR**
> - Apple Upgrade is now live in the United States
> - Klarna-backed leasing for most iPhone, Mac, iPad and Apple Watch models
> - Replaces new enrollments in the old iPhone Upgrade Program
> - No AppleCare+ included

## 📈 Official launch

Apple has officially launched **Apple Upgrade**, its new device leasing program powered by Klarna. The service went live in the U.S. on July 28, 2026, and is available both online and in Apple retail stores.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Launch date | July 28, 2026 |
| Partner | Klarna |
| Eligible devices | Most iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch (entry-level models excluded) |
| Terms | 24 months (iPhone/Watch) · 36 months (Mac/iPad) |
| AppleCare+ | Not included |

## 💰 How it works

- Soft credit check required
- Lower monthly payments than traditional financing
- At the end of the term (or earlier) you can upgrade, return the device, or buy it out
- Some early actions may carry extra fees
- You must fully erase personal data before returning any device

## ✅ Key differences vs old iPhone Upgrade Program

- Broader device coverage (not just iPhone)
- True lease structure instead of installment financing
- No bundled AppleCare+
- Backed by Klarna instead of Apple’s previous financing partner

## 🗓️ Who it’s for

Best suited for users who upgrade regularly and prefer lower monthly payments. Less ideal if you want to own the device outright or need AppleCare included.
