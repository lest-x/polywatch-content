> **TL;DR**
> - AppleCare One expands to Australia, France, Germany and the UK on August 4
> - One monthly plan covers up to three devices
> - Pairs well with the new Apple Upgrade leasing program launching July 28

## 📈 What’s changing

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| New countries | Australia, France, Germany, United Kingdom |
| Launch date | August 4, 2026 |
| Coverage | Up to 3 devices under one plan |
| Existing markets | Continues in the United States and other earlier markets |

Apple is expanding its flexible AppleCare One subscription to four additional countries. The plan lets customers protect multiple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, etc.) under a single monthly payment instead of buying individual AppleCare+ plans.

## 💰 How it works with Apple Upgrade

The new **Apple Upgrade** leasing program (powered by Klarna) launches in the U.S. on July 28 and does **not** include AppleCare+. That makes AppleCare One (or standard AppleCare+) an important add-on for anyone who leases.

Key points:
- AppleCare One can cover devices obtained through leasing
- Monthly cost is often lower than three separate AppleCare+ plans
- Theft and loss coverage options vary by country

Exact local pricing will appear on Apple’s regional sites closer to August 4.

## ✅ Who should consider it

- Users with two or more Apple devices
- Anyone joining the Apple Upgrade leasing program who wants protection without buying separate plans
- Customers who prefer predictable monthly costs over one-time AppleCare+ fees

## 🗓️ Timeline

- July 28: Apple Upgrade leasing launches in the U.S.
- August 4: AppleCare One goes live in Australia, France, Germany and the UK
