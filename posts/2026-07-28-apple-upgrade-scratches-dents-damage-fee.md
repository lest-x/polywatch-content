> **TL;DR**
> - Minor scratches and small dents are usually accepted as normal wear
> - Cracked screens, deep dents, or functional damage can trigger a fee
> - AppleCare+ is the best protection against damage fees

## 📈 The official rule

When you return a device leased through **Apple Upgrade**, it must be in **“good working condition.”**

Apple and Klarna accept **normal wear and tear** and **minor cosmetic imperfections**. Light surface scratches and small dents that do not affect how the phone works are generally fine.

## ✅ What is usually accepted

| Type of damage              | Likely outcome          |
|----------------------------|-------------------------|
| Light surface scratches    | Accepted (normal wear) |
| Small, shallow dents       | Accepted (normal wear) |
| Minor scuffs from daily use| Accepted               |
| No functional issues       | Accepted               |

## ❌ What can trigger a damage fee

- Cracked or broken screen
- Deep or sharp dents
- Broken buttons, charging port, or cameras
- Any damage that affects normal use

If the phone is judged beyond “good working condition” and you **do not have AppleCare**, Klarna will charge a **one-time damage fee** when the device is returned (whether you are upgrading or simply ending the lease).

## 🛡️ How to protect yourself

1. **Get AppleCare+** (or AppleCare One) within 60 days of starting the lease. This is the strongest protection against damage fees.
2. Use a good case and screen protector to minimize cosmetic wear.
3. If the phone has real damage, consider repairing it before returning it to avoid the fee.

## 🗓️ Bottom line

Minor scratches and small dents almost never cause problems.  
Anything more serious (especially screen damage) can result in a charge unless you have AppleCare coverage.

Returning the phone in good working condition keeps the process simple and fee-free.
