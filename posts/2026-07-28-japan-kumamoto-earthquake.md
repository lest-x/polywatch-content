## 📈 What happened

A strong earthquake hit southwestern Japan on July 28, 2026. The Japan Meteorological Agency reported a preliminary magnitude in the 6.8–7.1 range centered in Kumamoto Prefecture on the island of Kyushu.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Location | Central Kumamoto Prefecture |
| Magnitude | 6.8 – 7.1 (preliminary) |
| Tsunami | Alert issued for western Kyushu coast |
| Impact | Buildings and highways damaged; mall collapse reported |

## 🔥 Current situation

- Local broadcaster TBS reported a “considerable number” of people dead at a damaged shopping mall (Aeon Mall area mentioned in early reports)
- Tsunami advisory in effect for parts of the western Kyushu coastline
- Multinational and local rescue teams active
- Infrastructure damage including roads and buildings

Authorities are assessing the full extent of casualties and damage. Residents in coastal areas were advised to move to higher ground while the tsunami alert remained active.

## 🗓️ What to watch

- Updated death and injury tolls
- Cancellation or continuation of the tsunami alert
- Aftershock activity
- Government emergency response level
