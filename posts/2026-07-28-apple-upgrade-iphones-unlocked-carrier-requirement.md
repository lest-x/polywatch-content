> **TL;DR**
> - Apple Upgrade iPhones require choosing AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon at enrollment
> - The phones remain **unlocked** — you can switch carriers later
> - This is different from the recent carrier-installment locking policy

## 📈 The confusion

Some early reports and social media posts suggested that iPhones obtained through the new Apple Upgrade leasing program (powered by Klarna) would be permanently locked to AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon. That is not the case.

## ✅ Official details

According to Apple’s own Apple Upgrade page and hands-on coverage from 9to5Mac and AppleInsider:

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Carrier at signup | Must select AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon (no prepaid) |
| Device status | **Unlocked** |
| Switching later | Allowed at any time |
| Other devices | iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch have no carrier requirement |

You only need to connect the iPhone to one of the three major carriers when you first enroll in the lease. After activation, the device behaves like a normal unlocked iPhone.

## 🔄 How this differs from carrier financing

In mid-July 2026 Apple closed a previous loophole: iPhones financed through T-Mobile or Verizon installment plans purchased on Apple’s site are now carrier-locked until fully paid off (matching AT&T’s long-standing policy).

Apple Upgrade is a separate Klarna-backed **lease**, not a carrier installment plan, so the unlocking rules are different.

## 🗓️ Bottom line

- Yes, you must pick a major carrier to start an iPhone lease.
- No, the phone is not locked to that carrier.
- You retain the flexibility to change networks later.

This makes Apple Upgrade more flexible than traditional carrier financing for users who value the ability to switch providers.
