Lede: the month in one stat (total whale volume tracked, biggest single
trade, most violent repricing).

## The list

Ranked entries. Each: date, market, trade size and side, price impact,
what happened next (did the market resolve? did the move hold?). One short
paragraph each — the numbers are the story.

## Patterns this month

2–3 observations the list supports: which categories drew size, whether
big trades led or followed price, hit rate of whale direction vs resolution
where known. Only claims the data in the list actually supports.

## Methodology

How PolyWatch detects these (detector thresholds in plain words), what
"whale trade" means here, and what the data can't show (identity, intent).
Journalists cite pieces with methodology sections; that is the point.
