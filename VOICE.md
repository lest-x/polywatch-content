# PolyWatch editorial voice

Read this before drafting anything. Every rule here exists to keep the blog
credible with a BS-allergic audience and onside with the analytics-not-betting
positioning that keeps the app in the stores.

## Voice

- **Sharp, data-first, zero hype.** Numbers beat adjectives. "YES moved 59¢ →
  71¢ in 40 minutes on $180K of volume" beats "the market exploded."
- **Slightly insider, never condescending.** Assume the reader is smart and
  busy. No filler intros ("In today's fast-paced world…"), no padded
  conclusions.
- **Prices in cents, moves in cents-and-minutes.** "64¢", "+3¢ in 15 min".
  Translate to probability where it aids comprehension: "64¢ — a 64% implied
  probability."
- **Plain verbs.** Markets *move*, *reprice*, *resolve*. Nobody "skyrockets."

## Hard rules (CI enforces some of these)

1. **Analytics framing only.** Describe what the market is pricing and how it
   moved. Never advise. Banned: "bet on", "should buy", "you should sell",
   "guaranteed", "can't lose", "free money", "get rich", "make money with".
2. **Never fabricate a number.** Every figure must come from the data digest
   or a cited source. If the digest lacks it, omit it — an article with three
   real numbers beats one with ten invented ones.
3. **No headline bait.** Titles state what the piece shows. British-tabloid
   headlines are banned.
4. **Attribute uncertainty honestly.** "A single wallet took $50K of YES —
   informed flow or a hedge, the tape doesn't say" is a *good* sentence.
5. **Not affiliated with Polymarket** — never imply otherwise.

## Structure

- Daily Radar: 400–700 words. Guides: 1,200–2,000. Event briefs: 600–1,000.
- No H1 in the body — the site renders `title` as the H1. Start at `##`.
- H2s should contain natural keyword variants (searchers' phrasing).
- ≥ 2 internal links per post (other posts or polywatch.org pages).
- End with **one factual CTA line** — what the app does, not a promise.
  Good: "PolyWatch pushes whale trades like these the moment they land —
  free on iOS and Android." Bad: "Never miss out on profits again!"

## SEO mechanics

- `title` ≤ 60 chars, primary keyword in the first half.
- `description` 140–160 chars, one sentence, contains the keyword, no clickbait.
- One target query per post; variants go in H2s, not stuffed into paragraphs.
